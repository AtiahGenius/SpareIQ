# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want SpareIQ to be built by a team where people do their best work. That means treating every contributor — teammates, reviewers, and anyone reporting an issue — with respect, regardless of experience level, background, or role.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews, issues, and discussions. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and give people the benefit of the doubt in written communication.
- Be welcoming to newcomers — everyone was new to this codebase once.
- Give credit where it's due, and be generous in reviews with what's working, not just what needs to change.
- Keep disagreements focused on the technical or product decision at hand.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments directed at a contributor.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Dismissing or belittling someone's contribution without constructive feedback.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository (issues, PRs, code review comments), and any team channels used to discuss the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please raise it with a project maintainer directly. Reports will be handled discreetly and taken seriously.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove comments, commits, or contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, or temporarily/permanently restrict access for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
